Our services

A full range of wall, ceiling and partition finishes, from metal stud framing to the final coat.

01

Metal Stud Partitions

Metal stud partitions offer a cost-effective, lightweight, and versatile solution for dividing internal spaces. These non-load-bearing partitions are suitable for both domestic and commercial projects, providing flexibility, durability, and excellent performance.

02

Tape & Jointing

Tape and jointing delivers smooth, flawless plasterboard walls and ceilings ready for decoration. Our machine-applied jointing system is completed in three stages, ensuring a consistent, high-quality finish with minimal sanding and fast turnaround times.

03

Drylining

Drylining provides a simple and efficient solution for lining timber ceilings and walls, metal stud partitions, and masonry walls. Suitable for new-build, refurbishment, and commercial projects, drylining offers a clean, modern finish while improving thermal and acoustic performance.

04

Rendering

We install high-quality through-coloured render systems designed to provide long-lasting, low-maintenance weather protection. Available in a range of colours and textures, rendering enhances the appearance of external facades while protecting the building from the elements.

05

Plastering

Plastering is the skilled application of plaster to interior walls and ceilings, creating smooth, level surfaces ready for decoration.

Plaster skimming: A thin finishing coat of plaster applied to new plasterboard or existing plastered surfaces to create a perfectly smooth finish.
Float and skim (two-coat plastering): An initial backing coat of plaster is applied to level uneven surfaces, followed by a finishing skim coat to achieve a smooth, durable finish.
06

Suspended Ceilings

Suspended ceilings offer a cost-effective and lightweight solution for both acoustic performance and aesthetic design. Systems can be finished with lay-in grid ceiling tiles, available in a wide range of colours and styles, or lined with plasterboard to create a seamless appearance.
